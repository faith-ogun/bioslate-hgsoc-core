# ABCB8 – H2BC8 Gene Pair: Structured Report

## 1. Background on Genes

### ABCB8
ABCB8, a member of the ATP-binding cassette (ABC) transporter family, is involved in the transport of peptides and various molecules across membranes. It has been implicated in mitochondrial iron homeostasis and has a potential role in chemoresistance as observed in multiple studies.

**Relevance to Cancer:**
- Alteration in the copy number of ABCB8 has been associated with acquired drug resistance in cancer, highlighting its role in chemoresistance mechanisms (PMID: 14973057).

### H2BC8
H2BC8 is a histone protein variant that participates in chromatin remodeling and gene expression regulation.

**Relevance to Cancer:**
- Dysregulated expression of H2BC8 has been associated with the progression of cancer, particularly within the context of pyroptosis-related gene sets in papillary thyroid cancer (PMID: 38287330).
