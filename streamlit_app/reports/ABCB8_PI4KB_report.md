# Structured Report on Gene Pair: ABCB8 – PI4KB

## 1. Background on Genes

### ABCB8
ABCB8 (ATP Binding Cassette Subfamily B Member 8) has shown relevance in multiple myeloma (MM) through mechanisms involving long non-coding RNA NEAT1, as it plays a role in assisting tumor progression via interactions with miR-485-5p, leading to increased proliferation and reduced apoptosis of cancer cells (PMID: 39475764).

### PI4KB
PI4KB (Phosphatidylinositol 4-Kinase Beta) is implicated in cancer, particularly in non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC), as it recruits oncogenic proteins through the Golgi, affecting cancer cell growth and metastasis (PMID: 40189704). It is also involved in autophagy processes important for the survival of RAS-mutant cancer cells (PMIDs: 40055523, 39988733).

## 6. Conclusion

Current evidence suggests that while ABCB8 and PI4KB individually hold importance in certain cancers, particularly in hematological malignancies and lung cancer, there is limited direct evidence of their interaction or significant impact through synthetic lethality across more cancer types, including ovarian and breast cancer. Future research should focus on elucidating any potential interactions further and exploring therapeutic strategies exploiting these genes.
