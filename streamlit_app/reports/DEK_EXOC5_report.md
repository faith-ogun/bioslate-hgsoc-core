# DEK – EXOC5 Gene Pair: A Structured Report

## 1. Background on Genes

**DEK:** DEK is an oncogenic chromatin remodeling protein known for its role in various cancers. It has been implicated in promoting mammary hyperplasia and affects epigenetic modifications associated with breast cancer (PMID: [40562540](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40562540)). Furthermore, DEK amplification is linked to the advancement of peritoneal metastasis in gastric cancer, highlighting its role in tumor progression and potential as a therapeutic target (PMID: [40436141](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40436141)). In ovarian cancer, DEK is overexpressed and associated with poor survival, suggesting its influence on apoptotic pathways and tumorigenesis (PMID: [40392449](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40392449)).

**EXOC5:** EXOC5 is a component of the exocyst complex involved in vesicular trafficking, ciliogenesis, and renal development (PMID: [30824539](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30824539)). However, there seems to be no direct evidence linking EXOC5 to synthetic lethality (SL) or cancer therapies based on the available PubMed search data.
