## Comprehensive Markdown Report: ABCF2 – PI4KB Gene Pair

### 1. Background on Genes

**ABCF2**: 
ABCF2 is a member of the ATP-binding cassette (ABC) family, primarily involved in multidrug resistance (MDR) mechanisms within cancer types, notably ovarian cancer. Overexpression of ABCF2 has been linked to poor prognosis and chemoresistance, particularly regarding cisplatin resistance in ovarian cancer (PMID: 28112439). Additionally, it is identified as a potential prognostic biomarker in hepatocellular carcinoma with vascular invasion and bladder cancer (HCC and BLCA) (PMIDs: 39114683, 37960960). 

**PI4KB**: 
Phosphatidylinositol 4-kinase beta (PI4KB) is known for its role in producing phosphatidylinositol 4-phosphate, a lipid molecule crucial for membrane trafficking and signaling. PI4KB has been implicated in non-canonical autophagy in RAS-mutated cancers, suggesting it as a potential therapeutic target (PMID: 40055523). In lung cancer, PI4KB's recruitment facilitates oncogenic secretion influencing cancer cell growth (PMID: 40189704).

### 5. Translational Potential (across cancers)
