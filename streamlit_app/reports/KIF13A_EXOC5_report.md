# Report: Gene Pair KIF13A – EXOC5

## 1. Background on Genes

### KIF13A
KIF13A is a kinesin family member involved in intracellular transport, and emerging evidence suggests its potential role in cancer progression. In non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC), a KIF13A-RET fusion was reported in a patient who responded positively to targeted therapy with pralsetinib, highlighting its relevance as a biomarker (PMID: 39057153).

### EXOC5
EXOC5 is a component of the exocyst complex critical for various cellular processes, including protein trafficking and ciliogenesis. Altered EXOC5 expression and function are linked to cellular structural changes and have implications in cancer metastasis, particularly in the context of breast cancer, where its dysregulation affects E-cadherin trafficking and contributes to the formation of lymphovascular emboli (PMID: 22638108, PMID: 21036701).
