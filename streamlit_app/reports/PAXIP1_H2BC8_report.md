# PAXIP1 – H2BC8 Gene Pair Report

## 1. Background on Genes

**PAXIP1**: The PAX-interacting protein 1 (PAXIP1) plays a significant role in the development of multiple cancer types. Recent studies have highlighted its involvement in hepatocellular carcinoma, lung cancer and its potential as a prognostic biomarker in ovarian cancer. PAXIP1 has been linked to immune modulation and expression levels inversely correlate with survival outcomes in these cancer types.

**H2BC8**: H2BC8 is identified as one of the pyroptosis-related genes with implication in papillary thyroid cancer, indicating a potential involvement in tumor immune microenvironment remodeling. However, specific roles of H2BC8 in cancer outside of its involvement in pyroptosis have not been fully established.
