# Synthetic Lethality and Drug Target Report: CDK5 – H2BC8

## 1. Background on Genes

### CDK5
CDK5, a cyclin-dependent kinase, plays a crucial role in the central nervous system. It is atypical for its family, as it does not typically regulate the cell cycle. However, CDK5's dysregulation has been implicated in various cancers due to its involvement in pathways that regulate cellular migration, proliferation, and angiogenesis. Several studies highlight CDK5 as a significant oncogenic driver and a potential therapeutic target, as evidenced by molecular docking studies and experimental assays focusing on its inhibition (PMID: 40606194, PMID: 40591463).

### H2BC8
Currently, H2BC8 does not have established relevance as a cancer biomarker or therapeutic target. This gene's role in synthetic lethality studies or cancer contexts has not been supported by available PubMed data.
