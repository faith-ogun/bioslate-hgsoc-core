## 1. Background on Genes

### ZBTB20
ZBTB20, or Zinc Finger and BTB Domain Containing 20, is a transcription factor recognized for its role in cellular growth regulation and development. It has been identified in various cancer contexts, although specific evidence relating to its function as a direct biomarker in other cancer types remains limited. For instance, ZBTB20 has a notable role in macrophage polarization within the tumor microenvironment, specifically in triple-negative breast cancer, where it influences macrophage activity and potentially impacts tumor growth ([PMID: 40580873](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40580873)).

### ACLY
Adenosine triphosphate citrate lyase (ACLY) is a critical enzyme involved in lipid biosynthesis and is particularly relevant in carcinogenesis due to its role in metabolic reprogramming, a hallmark of cancer. ACLY overexpression is associated with poor prognosis in cancers such as ovarian cancer, where it contributes to cisplatin resistance ([PMID: 33816292](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33816292)).
