# Structured Report on Gene Pair: CNOT7 – TAFAZZIN

## 1. Background on Genes

### CNOT7
CNOT7 (CCR4-NOT Transcription Complex Subunit 7) is a subunit of the CCR4-NOT complex, which plays a role in mRNA degradation and transcription regulation. CNOT7 has been implicated in the progression of glioma and ovarian cancer. Studies have shown that CNOT7 is highly expressed in gliomas and associated with poor prognosis. It influences cancer development through multiple pathways such as IL6-JAK-STAT3 signaling (PMID: 38985240). In ovarian cancer, CNOT7 modulates cell proliferation and survival via the AKT signaling pathway, demonstrating its potential as a therapeutic target (PMID: 33412213).

### TAFAZZIN
TAFAZZIN, associated with Barth syndrome, is crucial for phospholipid remodeling in the mitochondrial membrane. While primarily linked with inherited metabolic disorders, TAFAZZIN impacts cancer biology through its role in cell membrane composition and energy metabolism, specifically in chemoresistance mechanisms in breast cancer mediated by miR-125a-5p (PMID: 34549308).
