# TMUB1 – H2BC8 Gene Pair in Cancer Contexts

## 1. Background on Genes

### TMUB1
TMUB1 (Transmembrane and Ubiquitin-like Domain Containing 1) has been implicated in cancer contexts, particularly in its regulatory role in the apoptosis pathway. According to a study, TMUB1 enhances apoptosis in TP53 mutation-independent settings, indicating its interaction with mutant forms of p53 and affecting apoptotic gene expression (PMID: 38731819). Additionally, it has been shown that TMUB1 is involved in the progression of colorectal cancer through the TMUB1/AKT pathway, highlighting its relevance in tumor proliferation and metastasis, particularly in smokers (PMID: 38341886).

### H2BC8
The role of H2BC8 in cancer is less clear; however, research suggests its involvement in papillary thyroid cancer (PTC) progression as part of a pyroptosis-related gene signature. This gene is linked to the tumor immune microenvironment and could be influential in deriving prognostic models for PTC patients (PMID: 38287330).
