# VPS37A – TAFAZZIN: Comprehensive Cancer Report

## 1. Background on Genes
### VPS37A
VPS37A, also known as HCRP1, is a component of the endosomal sorting complexes required for transport (ESCRT) machinery. It plays a crucial role in the regulation of receptor degradation, particularly for tyrosine kinase receptors such as EGFR. Loss of HCRP1 has been associated with poor prognosis and increased metastatic potential in epithelial cancers, as well as resistance to cetuximab in ovarian cancer (PMID: 22016507, PMID: 28122307).

### TAFAZZIN
TAFAZZIN is involved in the remodeling of cardiolipin, a mitochondrial phospholipid essential for mitochondrial function. Inherited mutations in TAFAZZIN lead to Barth Syndrome, characterized by mitochondrial dysfunction, metabolic anomalies, and cardiomyopathy. Experimental models have observed metabolic and inflammatory changes with TAFAZZIN deficiency, though these have not directly linked to cancer contexts (PMID: 40603434).
