## 1. Background on Genes

### M6PR (Mannose-6-Phosphate Receptor)
M6PR is involved in trafficking hydrolase enzymes to lysosomes. It potentially plays a role in cancer as a pathway to exploit for targeted protein degradation, as evidenced by its use in lysosome-targeting chimeras (LYTACs) to degrade membrane proteins in cancer therapy. While M6PR's association with synthetic lethality (SL) is not documented, its role in targeting proteins for lysosomal degradation suggests it might have future applicability in cancer therapeutics.

### LAMTOR2 (Late Endosomal/Lysosomal Adaptor, MAPK and MTOR Activator 2)
LAMTOR2 is part of the Ragulator complex that regulates the mechanistic target of rapamycin complex 1 (mTORC1) pathway, which is crucial for cell growth and metabolism. In the context of cancer, LAMTOR2 is linked to cancer progression, as shown in studies highlighting its interaction with mTOR signaling pathways, enhancing certain malignancies like pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC).
