# NUP153 – EXOC5 Gene Pair Report

## 1. Background on Genes

### NUP153
Nucleoporin 153 (NUP153) is a member of the nucleoporin family, which plays a key role in nucleocytoplasmic transport—a critical step in the regulation of gene expression and signal transduction. NUP153 has been implicated in several cancers due to its involvement in processes such as cell cycle regulation and interaction with the tumor microenvironment. Notably, high expression of NUP153 is associated with poor prognosis in various cancers, including cholangiocarcinoma, colorectal, and gastric cancers. Elevated NUP153 levels correlate with increased tumor-associated macrophage infiltration, suggesting a role in modulating immune cell dynamics within tumors (PMID: 40607419).

### EXOC5
EXOC5, a component of the exocyst complex, is involved in targeting and docking exocytic vesicles at the plasma membrane, a process essential for vesicular trafficking. Alterations in EXOC5 expression are linked to breast cancer, highlighting its role in tumor emboli formation and metastasis. EXOC5's function within the primary cilium is critical for ciliogenesis, and its dysregulation can alter cellular processes that contribute to oncogenesis (PMID: 30824539).
