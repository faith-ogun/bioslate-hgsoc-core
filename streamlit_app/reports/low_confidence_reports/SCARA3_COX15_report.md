# Synthetic Lethality and Cancer Relevance: SCARA3 – COX15

## 1. Background on Genes

### SCARA3
SCARA3, or Scavenger Receptor Class A Member 3, is known for its role in immune response and cellular stress responses. Recent studies highlight its overexpression in ovarian carcinoma compared to breast carcinoma, suggesting potential implications in cancer biology and tumor progression (PMID: 21855113). Specifically, SCARA3 mRNA levels have been associated with disease recurrence post-chemotherapy in ovarian cancer, indicating a potential biomarker role in cancer progression and treatment response.

### COX15
COX15 is involved in the biosynthesis of heme a, a critical component of cytochrome c oxidase, which plays a fundamental role in cellular respiration (PMID: 37348627). Although specific roles in cancer have not been extensively documented, altered mitochondrial functions associated with COX15 may contribute to cancer-related metabolic adaptations.
