## 1. Background on Genes

### POGZ
The pogo transposable element-derived zinc finger protein (POGZ) is associated with various tumorigenic processes. Particularly, studies have highlighted its role in suppressing metastasis in triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC) by attenuating TGFβ signaling, indicating it may serve both as a tumor promoter and a metastasis suppressor depending on context [PMID: 39137399](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39137399). Further research has also suggested a role for POGZ in modulating thyroid cancer progression through interactions with MAD2L2 [PMID: 40071126](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40071126).

### PARD6B
Partitioning Defective 6B (PARD6B) is involved in establishing epithelial cell polarity. In colorectal cancer, it has been observed to promote tumor growth by increasing MYC expression, making it a potential prognostic marker [PMID: 40533910](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40533910). PARD6B also interacts with the MAPK signaling pathway, relevant in triple-negative breast cancer contexts [PMID: 38823535](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38823535).
