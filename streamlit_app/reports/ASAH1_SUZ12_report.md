## 1. **Background on Genes**

### ASAH1
ASAH1, or Acid Ceramidase, is a gene involved in sphingolipid metabolism, which plays roles in regulating cellular processes such as cell proliferation and apoptosis. It has been evaluated for its prognostic value in several cancers. For example, ASAH1's involvement in ovarian cancer through histotype-specific expression denotes its significance in cancer prognosis and potential as a biomarker (PMID: 31897818).

### SUZ12
SUZ12 is a component of the Polycomb Repressive Complex 2 (PRC2) and is implicated in cancer through its role in chromatin remodeling and gene repression. It has been studied in various cancer contexts, including its role in prostate cancer and endometrial stromal tumors (ESTs) where it is involved in the JAZF1-SUZ12 rearrangement, a hallmark of ESTs (PMID: 40522594, PMID: 36842409).
