# ZBTB20 – TTF2 Gene Pair Report

## 1. Background on Genes

### ZBTB20
ZBTB20 is a transcription factor involved in various cellular processes, including development and differentiation. It has been implicated in the modulation of immune response through macrophage polarization, particularly in triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC). In this context, ZBTB20 has been shown to reprogram tumor microenvironment, promoting an M1 macrophage phenotype, which is associated with an immune-promotive, tumor-suppressive environment (PMID: 40580873).

### TTF2
Transcription Termination Factor 2 (TTF2) plays a role in mitotic transcriptional silencing and cell cycle regulation. Overexpression of TTF2 has been noted in a variety of solid tumors, correlating with poor patient survival. TTF2’s regulation of the cell cycle involves interaction with key mitotic players, affecting cell proliferation and potentially promoting tumorigenesis (PMID: 40410652).
