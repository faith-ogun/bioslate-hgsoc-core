**VPS37A**: VPS37A, known as Hepatocellular Carcinoma-related Protein 1 (HCRP1), is a component of the ESCRT-I complex, which is involved in endosomal sorting and degradation of membrane receptors. It has been linked to several cancers, such as breast, ovarian, and hepatocellular carcinoma. Loss or downregulation of VPS37A has been implicated in tumor progression and poor prognosis due to enhanced activation of pathways like EGFR and induction of epithelial-mesenchymal transition (EMT).  
**SUZ12**: SUZ12 is a component of the Polycomb Repressive Complex 2 (PRC2), which is responsible for tri-methylation of histone H3 on lysine 27, leading to transcriptional repression of target genes. Somatic mutations in SUZ12 have been associated with several cancers, and its dysregulation can contribute to tumor progression and metastasis.
