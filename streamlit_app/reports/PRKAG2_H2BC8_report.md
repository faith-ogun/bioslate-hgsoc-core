## 1. Background on Genes

### PRKAG2
The PRKAG2 gene encodes a regulatory subunit of AMP-activated protein kinase (AMPK). AMPK acts as an energy sensor in cells, regulating metabolic pathways to maintain energy homeostasis. Mutations in PRKAG2 have been linked with metabolic disorders, including a glycogen storage disease characterized by cardiac complications. The role of PRKAG2 in cancer is not explicitly documented in the provided references, indicating weak evidence for its relevance as a biomarker in oncology contexts.

### H2BC8
H2BC8 is a histone cluster member implicated in the regulation of DNA and chromatin structure. Its specific role in cancer development or progression is not well established in the supplied data, though its involvement in pyroptosis, a form of programmed cell death, in papillary thyroid cancer (PTC) has been investigated (PMID: 38287330).
