# ZNF687 – PARD6B Gene Pair: A Structured Analysis

## Background on Genes

### ZNF687
ZNF687, predominantly implicated in hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC), exhibits overexpression associated with shorter overall survival and resistance to tyrosine kinase inhibitors (TKIs) in HCC patients. Research has suggested ZNF687 as a potential target for therapeutic interventions, highlighting its role in treatment resistance and as a biomarker for poor prognostic outcomes. Notably, chaetocin has been identified as a candidate therapeutic compound targeting ZNF687 [PMID: 39377570](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39377570).

### PARD6B
PARD6B, a polarity gene, plays a critical role in colorectal cancer (CRC) by upregulating MYC expression, supporting tumor growth and poor prognosis. Enhanced expression of PARD6B correlates with DNA copy number amplification in CRC, making it a potential therapeutic target. Additionally, in ovarian cancer (OC), PARD6B is linked to chemoresistance, influencing treatment efficacy and patient survival [PMID: 40533910](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40533910) and [PMID: 39262489](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39262489).
