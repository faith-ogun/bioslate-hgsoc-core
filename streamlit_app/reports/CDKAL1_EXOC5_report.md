# CDKAL1 – EXOC5 Gene Pair Report

## 1. Background on Genes

**CDKAL1 (CDK5 Regulatory Subunit-Associated Protein 1 Like 1):**  
CDKAL1 is implicated in metabolic functions and its genetic variants are associated with adverse pregnancy outcomes, notably within populations with gestational diabetes mellitus (PMID: 40133860). Interestingly, CDKAL1 is also highlighted as a differentiator between cisplatin-resistant and -sensitive ovarian cancer, suggesting a role in oncogenic processes and therapeutic resistance (PMID: 39103807). Moreover, it is suggested that CDKAL1 contributes to the progression of hepatocellular carcinoma by being regulated through CDC123 (PMID: 38237400).

**EXOC5 (Exocyst Complex Component 5):**  
EXOC5 is a critical component of the exocyst complex, playing essential roles in intracellular processes such as protein trafficking and ciliogenesis. It is notably involved in renal ciliogenesis and cystogenesis (PMID: 30824539). In the context of cancer, alterations in EXOC5 have been linked to the formation of tumor emboli that exhibit resistance to chemotherapy, specifically in breast cancer models (PMID: 22638108, PMID: 21036701).
