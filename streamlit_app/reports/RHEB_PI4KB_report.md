# Report on the Gene Pair RHEB – PI4KB

## 1. Background on Genes

**RHEB (Ras Homolog Enriched in Brain):**  
RHEB is a member of the Ras superfamily of GTPases, primarily involved in the activation of mTORC1 (mechanistic target of rapamycin complex 1), which is crucial for cell growth and proliferation. Although RHEB's role in cancer is not exhaustively detailed within the provided abstracts, its involvement in ovarian cancer is mentioned, with RHEB being a part of autophagy-related gene networks influencing prognosis and immune microenvironment in cancer (PMID: 35941978).

**PI4KB (Phosphatidylinositol 4-Kinase Beta):**  
PI4KB plays a significant role in autophagy and has been studied in the context of cancer cell metabolism and survival, particularly under stress conditions, such as those induced by KRAS mutations. It has been implicated in a form of autophagy distinct from the classical pathway, potentially offering a target for RAS-mutant cancer therapies (PMID: 40055523, PMID: 39988733).
